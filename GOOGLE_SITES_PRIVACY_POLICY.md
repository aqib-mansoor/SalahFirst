### 1. Introduction & Core Philosophy
SalahFirst is built with a strict **Privacy-First, Offline-First** philosophy. We believe that your worship, daily spiritual routine, and location are sacred and personal. 
* We do **NOT** track you.
* We do **NOT** require an account, registration, email, or password.
* We do **NOT** run third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or data broker scripts.
* We do **NOT** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

### 2. Information We Access & How It Is Used
All computations are executed locally on your device:

#### A. Location Information (Approximate & Precise)
* **Purpose:** To calculate accurate Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the Qibla compass bearing towards the Kaaba in Makkah.
* **Storage & Transmission:** Your GPS coordinates are processed solely in temporary device memory. Your coordinates are **never transmitted to external servers**, never stored in remote databases, and never shared with third parties.
* **Manual Override:** You may decline GPS permission and manually choose a city from our offline bundled database.

#### B. Notification Permission
* **Purpose:** To deliver scheduled Azan alarms, pre-prayer reminders, and optional daily Quran/Hadith reflections.
* **Operation:** All notifications are scheduled locally on your device using the Android Notification Manager. No push notification servers or cloud messaging brokers receive your prayer schedule.

#### C. Storage & Media Library (Write-Only)
* **Purpose:** To allow you to save high-definition authentic Hadith wallpapers to your device's photo gallery upon your explicit request.
* **Operation:** SalahFirst uses write-only access to save images. We do **not** read, scan, or access your personal photos or documents.

#### D. Sensors (Magnetometer)
* **Purpose:** To operate the 3D Qibla compass needle. Sensor readings remain strictly in volatile device memory while the Qibla screen is active.

### 3. Data Retention & Deletion
Because SalahFirst does not maintain user accounts or cloud servers, all your prayer streaks, completed prayers, and bookmarks reside exclusively inside your device's local encrypted storage (`AsyncStorage`). 
* Uninstalling the application or clearing app data immediately and permanently erases all stored records.
* You can export an offline backup of your prayer history at any time from the app settings.

### 4. Children’s Privacy
SalahFirst does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
